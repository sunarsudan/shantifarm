Company Description

Manashanti Agriculture Farm is a family-owned agricultural enterprise established in 2071 B.S. with the vision of producing high-quality agricultural products through sustainable farming practices. The farm is located in Rukum, Tribeni–6, Nepal, and spreads across 60 ropani of fertile land.
The farm cultivates a wide variety of crops and fruits, including kiwi, oranges, apples, and rosemary, ensuring both diversity and quality in its agricultural production. In addition to crop farming, Manashanti Agriculture Farm is also involved in livestock farming, producing fresh cow milk and buffalo milk.
One of its unique products is rosemary oil, which is carefully produced using the steam distillation method. And it also uses modern tools such as a mini tiller for digging and farm preparation to improve efficiency.
The farm sells its products in both wholesale and retail markets. In addition to the local market, the farm also exports products to European countries and India (Krishnanagar). The farm is committed to providing high-quality natural agricultural products while maintaining sustainable farming practices.

Our Mission

Our mission is to produce fresh and high-quality agricultural products such as kiwi, oranges, apples, milk, and rosemary oil using effective farming methods and modern tools like mini tillers and steam distillation for rosemary oil production. As a family-owned farm, we aim to supply our products through both wholesale and retail markets locally and internationally while maintaining quality, sustainability, and customer trust.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted and leading agricultural farm known for sustainable farming excellence, innovation, and quality produce. We aim to contribute to a healthier society and a greener future by aligning agriculture with environmental sustainability.

Our History

2071 B.S. – Foundation

Manashanti Agriculture Farm was established in 2071 B.S. as a small family-owned farm in Rukum, Tribeni–6. The farm first started with the cultivation of rosemary and production of rosemary products, including rosemary oil.

2074 B.S. – Kiwi Introduction

After gaining good experience in rosemary farming, the farm expanded its production by introducing kiwi cultivation and increasing the variety of crops grown on the farm.

2076 B.S. – Apple Farming

Adopted smart farming practices and diversified crop varieties to improve productivity and environmental balance.

Recent Years – Growth and Recognition

Over time, the farm continued to grow by adding different crops and improving farming practices. Manashanti Agriculture Farm has also participated in agricultural exhibitions (Pradarshani programs) and received a Praman Patra (certificate of recognition) for its agricultural products and contributions.
