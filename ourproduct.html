🌿 Rosemary Oil

Our pure rosemary oil is extracted from high-quality, hand-picked leaves, preserving its fresh herbal fragrance. It is widely used for hair care, skin care, massage, and aromatherapy. Regular use promotes hair growth, strengthens roots, and reduces hair fall, while also aiding relaxation, reducing stress.It brings the natural benefits of this ancient herb to your daily wellness routine.

🌶 Akabare Chilli (Dalle Khursani)

Our Akabare chilli, also known as Dalle Khursani, is a round, bright red, extremely spicy chilli grown in hilly regions. It is rich in capsaicin and Vitamin C, known for its strong heat and unique flavor. it is perfect for pickles, chutneys, and traditional dishes, with high demand in local and international markets. Its vibrant color and fiery taste make it a must-have ingredient for authentic Nepali cuisine.

Rs.200 / kg

🌰 Sichuan Pepper (Timmur)

Timur, also known as Sichuan pepper, is a traditional spice known for its fresh citrus aroma and mild tingling sensation. Widely used in Nepali cuisine, it enhances the flavor of meat dishes, pickles, sauces, and spice blends. Grown in high-altitude regions, it is carefully harvested and naturally dried to preserve its essential oils. Beyond its unique taste, Timur is also valued for its digestive and medicinal benefits.

Rs.800 / kg

🌿 Rosemary Plant

Rosemary plants are perennial evergreen shrubs with woody stems and aromatic, needle-like leaves. They grow up to 1–1.5 m tall, with a main tap root and lateral roots. Thriving in sunny, well-drained soil, rosemary is drought-tolerant and easy to maintain, making it ideal for home gardens and herbal farms.

Rs.50 / Plant

🌶 Akabare Chilli (Dalle Khursani) Plant

Chilli shrubs are perennial and short-lived. They can grow up to 1.5 m in height. Their stems are woody at the base, fleshy and either erect or semi-prostrate. The shrub consists of a main tap root with many lateral roots. The leaves can grow up to 12 cm long and 7.5 cm wide and are unequal in shape with a pointed tip.

Rs.2.5 / Plant

🌰 Sichuan Pepper (Timmur) Plant

Timur plants are perennial shrubs growing up to 2–3 m tall, with woody stems at the base and green, fleshy young branches. They have a strong tap root with lateral roots. Leaves are pinnate, oval-shaped, 5–10 cm long, with pointed tips. The plant produces small, aromatic red berries used as a spice.

Rs.35 / Plant

🍋 Lemon Plant

Lemon plants are perennial evergreen shrubs or small trees that can grow up to 3–6 m tall. They have woody stems with green, flexible young branches and a main tap root with lateral roots. Leaves are glossy, ovate, 5–12 cm long, with pointed tips. The plants produce fragrant flowers and juicy, vitamin-rich fruits.

Rs.50 / Plant

🥝 Kiwi Plant

Kiwi plants are perennial climbing vines that can grow several meters in length with woody stems at the base and flexible young branches. They have a main tap root with lateral roots. Leaves are large, heart-shaped to oval, 8–15 cm long, with pointed tips. The plants produce fragrant flowers and sweet-tart, nutrient-rich fruits.

Rs.200 / Plant

🍎 Apple Plant

Apple plants are perennial deciduous trees that can grow up to 4–6 m tall. They have woody stems at the base and flexible young branches, with a main tap root and lateral roots. Leaves are ovateto elliptical, 5–12 cm long, 3–6 cm wide with pointed tips. The trees produce fragrant flowers and juicy, nutrient-rich fruits.

Rs.200 / Plant

🍊 Orange Plant

The tree has a strong main tap root with many lateral roots. Leaves are glossy, ovate to elliptical, 5–12 cm long, with pointed tips. Orange plants produce juicy and vitamin-rich fruits.
